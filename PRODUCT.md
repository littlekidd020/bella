# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Chinese-speaking male clients in Auckland, New Zealand, seeking premium companionship services. They browse primarily on mobile, often late evening, looking to view available companions, check details (photos, stats, services, pricing), and make contact via WeChat, WhatsApp, Telegram, or phone. Discretion and trust matter; the visual first-impression determines whether they stay or leave.

## Product Purpose
Angel Cuties (天使小可爱) is a high-end companion agency site for Auckland/NZ. The site showcases available companions with photos, descriptions, and pricing, and funnels visitors to contact channels (phone, WeChat, Telegram, WhatsApp). Success looks like: a visitor immediately trusts the brand, finds a companion they're interested in, and makes contact within 2-3 minutes.

## Brand Personality
Sweet, dreamy, angelic. The brand leans into a soft, feminine kawaii-adjacent aesthetic: pink tones, gentle gradients, anime-style illustrations, delicate typography. It should feel exclusive yet approachable, like a secret garden rather than a nightclub. Warm, not cold. Inviting, not intimidating.

## Anti-references
- Generic escort directory sites (harsh, utilitarian, no brand personality)
- Dark/gritty nightclub or adult industry aesthetics (neon, black backgrounds, aggressive imagery)
- Cheap or cluttered web designs with too many banners, pop-ups, or competing elements
- Overly corporate or sterile SaaS-style layouts

## Design Principles
1. **First impression is everything.** The site must feel premium and trustworthy within the first second. Every pixel communicates quality.
2. **Show, don't describe.** Let the companion photos and visual design do the heavy lifting. Minimize unnecessary text.
3. **Contact in three taps.** Every page should make it effortless to reach the agency. No dead ends.
4. **Bilingual by default.** Chinese and English coexist naturally. Neither language feels like an afterthought.
5. **Soft power.** The aesthetic whispers luxury, not shouts it. Elegance through restraint, warmth through color, trust through consistency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA minimum for text contrast
- Touch targets ≥44px for mobile (primary audience)
- Reduced motion support for animations
- Bilingual (Chinese/English) with proper font loading for both scripts
